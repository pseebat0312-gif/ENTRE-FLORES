# Original botanical assets

All active visual assets were independently generated for this project on 29 October 2026. No active asset was cropped from a website mockup or an asset grid.

- 17 individually generated transparent full-stem botanical PNGs; subjects are named in assets/flowers.
- 5 standalone floral arrangement photographs in assets/editorial.
- 4 standalone service and studio photographs.
- 1 courtyard garden photograph with a real wooden swing.
- 1 florist doorway photograph.
- 1 empty florist workbench photograph.
- 1 ivory ceramic pedestal vase with genuine alpha transparency.

Generation: built-in image generation. Botanical prompts requested one photorealistic stem per image on a transparent background, full stem visible, no text, no grid. Editorial prompts requested a single real photographic scene without UI or text. Generated masters remain separate from the optimized website files.

AI imagery is a creative interpretation rather than botanical identification evidence. Fonts: Fleur De Leah, Pinyon Script and EB Garamond from Google Fonts.

Additional originals: a denser continuous garden photograph, transparent photographic wooden swing, and transparent sculptural peony/rose foreground. These were individually generated for the dimensional update. The previous sparse garden photograph has been removed.

Seasonal extension: four separate garden images were generated from the same courtyard reference, preserving the path, arch and tree while changing the actual seasonal plants and atmosphere. Prompts: spring red/green/pink; summer emerald foliage with hydrangea/cosmos; autumn orange/copper flowers and leaves; winter white snow, frost and hellebores. Additional independent transparent assets: olive-glazed ceramic vase, amber ribbed glass vase, aged newspaper bouquet cone with wine ribbon, burnt-orange dahlia stem, white hellebore stem. All created with built-in image generation, optimized and copied into assets/seasons, assets/vessels and assets/flowers.
